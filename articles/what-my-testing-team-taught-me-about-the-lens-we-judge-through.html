What My Testing Team Taught Me About the Lens We Judge Through

A professional mindset can become a lens through which we judge everything — and that lens shapes how we live.

My first team was a testing team. Our job was simple: find what doesn't work. We walked into every project with the same implicit question — "Where's the mistake?" At the first meeting, a colleague told me, "We can't see what's positive. We're testers." That line hit me hard, because without realizing it, I'd started looking at the world the same way — hunting for flaws, risks, everything that could go wrong.

The same thing happened as a mother. I'd immediately spot what needed correcting, where I needed to intervene, what could go wrong. Roles train the mind — and if we're not careful, they become the lens we judge everything through. If you go through life looking for flaws, you'll find them — at work, in your kids, in yourself. A professional mindset shouldn't become an identity.

What did your job teach you that you carried home without meaning to?
