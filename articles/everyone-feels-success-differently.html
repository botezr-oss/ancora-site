Everyone Feels Success Differently

Success exists in two forms: outer and inner — and most people miss the recognition of what truly matters.

For me, success exists in two forms: inner and outer.

Outer success is easy to recognize — it comes from results, validation, feedback. A while back, I received this message from a client: her words described how our work together had improved her communication, given her more confidence, and helped her approach situations in a structured, grounded way. She'd become a Business Analysis trainer and had been proposed for a new role. She called it clear confirmation of the progress she'd made, and thanked me for seeing potential in her even when she wasn't sure of it herself.

That's the outer success. But the inner success was something else. It was the moment I watched her begin to trust her own way of thinking — to stop doubting herself at every step, to stop seeking validation before acting.

As a person and as a coach, I believe everyone carries a gift. But most of the time, what's missing isn't potential. What's missing is clarity. In my sessions, I'm not looking for quick fixes. I'm looking for the point where a person starts to see themselves differently — because that's where real change begins.

If you feel like you have more in you, but haven't yet found the words or the action for it, I'm curious: where do you feel most stuck?
