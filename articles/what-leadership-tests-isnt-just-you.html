What Leadership Tests Isn't Just You

Leadership doesn't test just the individual — it tests the couple, the family, the balance.

I was traveling abroad for work, in meetings that could change everything for my team. My phone kept ringing every thirty minutes — my husband: "Come home. The kids need you. I can't do this anymore." The pressure kept building; "come home" had turned into a reproach. And then I understood something painful: if I left now, I wasn't just stepping away from the trip. I was stepping away from myself.

I stayed. I finished the meetings. We won the project.

When I came back, I knew we couldn't keep functioning like that. Leadership doesn't just test you — it tests the couple, the family, the balance. Without alignment, every step forward professionally can become a fracture at home. Our relationship survived because we put everything on the table: his fear, my fear, the guilt, the ambition. Growth isn't individual. It's a whole system.

If you're in a leadership role and feel like you're paying the price at home, reach out. I work with women who want to be strong leaders without losing the connection to the people they love.
