The Mind in Business, the Soul with the Baby

How being fully present in two places at once became a lesson in divided attention and finding wholeness.

You left the house and your child was crying. Now you're at your desk, and you feel guilty that for one second, you felt relieved to breathe. You're juggling everything — roles, responsibilities, expectations — and still, by the end of the day, you feel like nothing was good enough. Maybe the hardest part isn't the exhaustion. It's that you're carrying all of it alone.

My son was four months old. I was nursing him, holding him close, feeling him with my whole heart — while my mind was in a conversation with a client, tuned into the nuances, into what wasn't being said, into where it hurt the most. The soul with the baby. The mind in business. Both real. Both part of me.

I'm certain mothers don't fail because they aren't enough. They struggle because no one ever gave them a space to be themselves — seen, supported, without judgment.

If this message feels like it's about you too, reach out — no pressure, no complicated forms. Just a conversation, in a safe space.
