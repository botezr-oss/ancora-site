Transformation Starts with Stillness, Clarity, and Truth

I offer a safe, reflective space for gentle but real transformation — one meaningful step at a time.

I'm Roxana Botez — a coach trained in coaching and in transformational coaching, holding space for those navigating moments of change, emotional complexity, or quiet inner conflict.

My practice is rooted in presence and emotional intelligence, guided by three essential forces: awareness, inner clarity, and aligned, conscious choice. I don't offer quick fixes. I offer a safe, reflective space for gentle but real transformation — one meaningful step at a time.

If you feel called to pause, listen inward, and move forward in truth, I'd love to meet you. A free, no-pressure exploratory session is available for those curious about what coaching with me feels like.
